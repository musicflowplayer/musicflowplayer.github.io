2. Description of Service

MusiFlow is an offline music player for audio files you already own or are lawfully entitled to use. The App allows you to:

  • Import your own audio files from your device, the iOS Files app, the in-app Wi-Fi import feature (works only over your local network), or Bluetooth transfer between your nearby devices;
  • Play your imported music, including background playback;
  • Create and manage personal playlists, favorites, and playback preferences;
  • Display lyrics from lyric files that you import yourself through the iOS document picker;
  • Trim up to 30 seconds of a song and export it as a ringtone file to share through the iOS share sheet.

No account or registration is required to use MusiFlow. The App is a playback tool for your own media and does not host, provide, download, or distribute any music content of its own.
