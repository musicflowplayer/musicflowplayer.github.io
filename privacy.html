Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

MusiFlow ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle when you use our music player application, and just as importantly, what we do not.

The short version: MusiFlow works entirely offline and does not collect your personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking, and the app sends no data to us or to any third-party server. Your music files, lyrics, playlists, and settings stay on your device. File imports happen only over your local network (Wi-Fi) or directly between nearby devices (Bluetooth peer-to-peer), and never touch the internet.

1. Introduction

MusiFlow is an offline music player for audio files you already own. It plays your imported music, manages playlists, displays lyrics from files you provide, lets you transfer songs between your own devices, and can export ringtones. By using MusiFlow, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Handle

MusiFlow is designed to minimize data handling. The following information is generated and used entirely on your own device, and is never uploaded to us or to any server we control:

  • Your music library and imported audio files — imported through the iOS Files app, the in-app Wi-Fi import feature (Section 5), or Bluetooth transfer from another of your devices (Section 6);
  • Lyrics files you import yourself for your tracks, and artwork from the metadata embedded in your own files (Section 7);
  • Playlists, favorites, and playback history;
  • App settings and preferences (including appearance and language);
  • In-app cache required for playback.

No account or registration is required to use MusiFlow. The app does not request microphone, camera, or location access, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. No Analytics, No Advertising, No Tracking

MusiFlow does not include any analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not engage in cross-app tracking. Specifically:

  • We do not collect device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA);
  • We do not request permission through Apple's AppTrackingTransparency framework;
  • We do not display advertisements or share data with advertising networks;
  • We do not use third-party analytics services to measure your behavior;
  • The app makes no requests to any internet server other than loading this legal page itself (Section 8).

4. Offline by Design

Everything MusiFlow does with your data happens on your device. Playback, playlists, lyrics display, ringtone export, and library management all work without an internet connection. The only network features in the app are local: Wi-Fi import on your own network and Bluetooth transfer between nearby devices, described below.

5. Wi-Fi Import (Local Network Only)

MusiFlow offers a Wi-Fi import feature for adding audio files from your computer. When you start it, the app runs a small HTTP server on your device and displays a local address you can open in your computer's browser on the same Wi-Fi network:

  • Files you upload are transferred directly from your computer to your device and are stored only in the app's local storage;
  • The import server listens on your local network only and is not reachable from the internet;
  • No import data ever passes through, or is visible to, us or any third party.

6. Bluetooth Transfer Between Nearby Devices

MusiFlow lets you send songs directly from one of your devices to another using peer-to-peer transfer (Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework, which combines Bluetooth and Wi-Fi). When you open the transfer screen:

  • The app searches for and connects only to nearby devices that also have this screen open, on a direct device-to-device connection;
  • Songs you send travel directly between the two devices — they are never uploaded to us, to any server, or to the internet;
  • The app only needs Bluetooth and local network permission for this feature; you can deny or revoke those permissions at any time in iOS Settings;
  • Discovery and transfer stop as soon as you leave the transfer screen.

7. Lyrics and Artwork

Lyrics shown in MusiFlow come only from lyric files that you import yourself through the iOS document picker. The app never downloads lyrics from the internet and never sends song information to any lyrics service. Album artwork comes from the metadata embedded in your own audio files and is stored locally.

8. The Only Online Content: This Legal Page

When you open the Privacy Policy or Terms of Service inside the app, an in-app browser loads this page (vibeflow-player.github.io) over HTTPS. This is the app's only internet request. It contains no information about you beyond the standard, anonymous connection your browser makes to any website; we do not receive any data from your library, playlists, or device.

9. Data Security

Your files and library data never leave your device except in the two local features above: Wi-Fi import, which stays within your own Wi-Fi network, and Bluetooth transfer, which travels directly between your paired devices over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection provided by iOS. No internet-facing transmission of your data exists in the app.

10. Local Data and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device, it remains under your control. You can delete imported tracks, lyrics, and cached content at any time from within the app. Uninstalling MusiFlow completely removes all data stored on your device — there is no server-side copy to delete because we never received one.

11. Children's Privacy

MusiFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, we do not collect data from children. If you believe otherwise, please contact us.

12. Your Rights and Choices

Since MusiFlow does not collect your personal data, there is no server-side profile to access, edit, or delete. You remain in full control through your device:

  • Manage local data: Review or delete your library, playlists, lyrics, and cache within the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Control Bluetooth and local network: Grant, deny, or revoke Bluetooth and local network access for Wi-Fi import and Bluetooth transfer at any time in iOS Settings; the rest of the app works fully without them.
  • Stop using the app: Uninstalling MusiFlow removes all locally stored data.
  • Contact us: For any privacy-related questions, reach out directly using the details below.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
